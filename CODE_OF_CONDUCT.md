# Community standards

Prosewire collaborators are expected to be respectful, specific, and
constructive in repository discussions, reviews, community spaces, and other
project-related communication.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, sustained personal attacks, and publication of another person's
private information are not acceptable. Critique ideas and contributions, not
the person making them.

## Reporting and enforcement

Raise conduct concerns privately with the maintainer at
[akshitkrnagpal@gmail.com](mailto:akshitkrnagpal@gmail.com). Include links or
other context needed to understand the incident, but do not send unrelated
credentials or private customer data.

Maintainers will review reports as privately as practical and may edit or
remove contributions, issue a warning, restrict participation, or take other
proportionate action to protect the project and its contributors. Retaliation
against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
