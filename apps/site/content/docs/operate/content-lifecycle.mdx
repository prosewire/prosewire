## States

- **Draft** — private and editable
- **Scheduled** — private until its publication time arrives
- **Published** — available to public readers and APIs
- **Archived** — removed from public listings
- **Restored** — returned to its prior working state with history intact

## Revisions and slugs

Prosewire creates revisions before destructive content changes. Slugs are unique within a blog, and when a published slug changes, the old public URL redirects to the new canonical URL.

Revision snapshots retain the editor and version for auditability. A dashboard history-and-restore interface is planned; today, restoring an archived post means changing its status through the editor or management API.

## Portable exports

The posts screen provides a versioned JSON export containing publication metadata, authors, categories, snippets, redirects, full posts, and revision snapshots. CSV remains available for spreadsheet interoperability.

## Discovery follows publication

Search, RSS, sitemap, related-post, and structured-data surfaces follow the same public visibility rule: content must be published and its publication time must have arrived.
